This Privacy Policy explains what information DuosApp collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the DuosApp Android app and this website. It is written to meet the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR where they apply, and Google Play's User Data policy.

1. The short version

  • DuosApp is a private app for two people. There is no public profile, no feed and no discovery.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your Google sign-in identity, the profile you create, the content you share with your partner, and — only if you turn it on — your location.
  • We do not sell your data, do not show ads, and do not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Your data is readable only by your account and your paired partner's account, enforced by server-side security rules.
  • You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

3. Why we use it

  • To run the app — deliver your messages and content to your partner, keep you signed in, send notifications, and sync across your devices.
  • To keep it private and safe — verify that requests come from a genuine copy of DuosApp (App Check), enforce that only the two paired accounts can read a chat, and investigate reports of abuse.
  • To provide Premium — verify purchases with Google Play and apply Premium to both partners.
  • To support you — respond to feedback and bug reports.
  • To meet legal obligations — including responding to lawful requests and our Child Safety Standards.

Under the GDPR, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you (Terms), our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, your consent (for location and notifications, which you can withdraw), and compliance with legal obligations. Under the DPDP Act, we process your data for the specified purposes above with your consent or for legitimate uses permitted by the Act.

4. Location sharing

  • Location sharing is off by default. You enable it from the Map tab, and Android asks you for permission at that point.
  • While it is on, DuosApp runs a foreground service (you'll see a persistent notification) and sends your precise location to our servers at intervals, where only your paired partner can read it.
  • We do not collect location in the background when sharing is off, and we never request "always-on" background location permission.
  • When you stop sharing — or the sharing session ends — your location record is deleted from our servers. We keep no history of where you have been.
  • You can revoke the location permission at any time in Android settings.

5. What your partner can see

Because DuosApp is a shared space, your partner can see the content you add to shared areas, your display name, photo, mood, birthday (day and month — and the year only if you choose to share it), and your location while you are sharing it.

The Secret Vault is shared too: you have one vault as a couple, either of you can view, add and delete anything in it, and the app shows who added each item. Your Vault PIN is not shared — each of you sets your own, and it never leaves your phone.

Your partner cannot see your Google account password, your Vault PIN, your device information or your purchase details.

6. Service providers and transfers

DuosApp is built on Google's Firebase platform. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments:

  • Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database (app data and presence), Cloud Storage for Firebase (photos, videos, voice notes, doodles, vault files), Cloud Functions (purchase verification, account deletion, Secret Vault keys and retention), Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications), Firebase App Check with Play Integrity (abuse prevention) and Firebase Remote Config (minimum-version checks).
  • Google Play Billing for purchases.

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure, which may be located outside your country, including outside India and the European Economic Area. Google maintains standard contractual safeguards for such transfers. We do not share your personal data with advertisers, data brokers or any other third party, and we only disclose it where the law requires or to protect someone's safety.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account and shared content: for as long as your account exists.
  • Location: only while sharing is on; deleted when it stops.
  • Deleted messages: "Delete for me" hides a message for you; "Delete for everyone" removes the content for both partners and leaves a placeholder.
  • After account deletion: your account, profile, location, mood and sign-in link are deleted at once. Content you shared with your partner (chat, doodles, diary and the rest of the shared space) stays available to them while they keep their account, with you shown as "Deleted Account", and is deleted when they delete theirs — the Data Deletion Policy sets this out in full. Residual copies in backups are removed within 30 days. We may retain minimal purchase records as required for tax and fraud prevention.
  • Secret Vault: items stay until one of you deletes them. If you unpair, shared access ends immediately; for the next 30 days each of you can still open, save and delete only the items you added yourself, and pairing with each other again within that time restores the whole vault. After 30 days the vault — its files, notes and its encryption key — is permanently deleted. If you delete your account, the Vault items you added are deleted immediately, and your partner has the same 30 days to save their own items before the vault is deleted. Nothing in the Vault is ever deleted to make room for new content.
  • On your phone: doodles and photos you have opened are kept on the device so they still open without a connection, and the titles and dates of your special dates are kept locally so reminders work offline. Vault items you add or open are kept on the phone only in encrypted form, and are left out of device backups; any decrypted copy made to show you a video or document is deleted as soon as the Vault locks. These copies never leave the phone; clear them any time from Settings → Storage, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months.

8. Security

  • All data travels over TLS (HTTPS) between the app and our servers.
  • Access is enforced by server-side security rules: every chat, photo and record is readable only by the two accounts in that pairing.
  • Every request carries a Play Integrity attestation (Firebase App Check), so our servers can tell a genuine, Play-installed copy of DuosApp from anything else and reject what isn't.
  • Secret Vault content — files, notes, titles and previews — is encrypted on your device with AES-256 (GCM) before it is uploaded. Each couple's encryption key is generated and stored on our servers and is released only to the two paired accounts, from a genuine copy of the app.
  • The Secret Vault is not end-to-end encrypted. Encryption protects the Vault if the stored files were ever exposed on their own, but because our servers hold the key, DuosApp could technically decrypt Vault content. The disclosure limits in section 6 apply to it like any other data.
  • Each partner opens the Vault with their own PIN (or fingerprint), which never leaves their phone. The Vault locks when you leave the app or stop using it, and screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside it. You can also add a PIN lock to the whole app.
  • No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: your profile and content are visible and editable in the app.
  • Deletion: Settings → Delete Account, or email us. See the Data Deletion Policy.
  • Location: switch sharing off in the app or revoke the permission in Android settings.
  • Notifications: manage them in Android settings or in the app.
  • Birth year: you decide whether it is stored and whether your partner sees it.
  • Portability, objection, restriction, withdrawal of consent, complaints: email us and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in India, the Data Protection Board).

10. Children

DuosApp is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at duosapp.support@gmail.com and we will delete it. Our approach to child safety is set out in our Child Safety Standards.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. The "Last updated" date will change, and material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.

12. Contact

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: duosapp.support@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 7 days.
