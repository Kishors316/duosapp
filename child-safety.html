These Child Safety Standards describe how DuosApp (published on Google Play by the DuosApp developer) prevents, detects and responds to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). They are published in line with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy and apply to the DuosApp Android app and this website.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services now. In India you can also call Childline on 1098.

1. Our commitment

DuosApp has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and for any conduct that sexualises, grooms, exploits or endangers children. We will remove such material when we become aware of it, terminate the accounts involved, preserve evidence, and report to the appropriate authorities.

2. Prohibited conduct and content

The following are prohibited everywhere in DuosApp — chat, doodles, moments, vault, diary, notes, games and all other areas — regardless of the age of the people in the pairing:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM): any visual depiction of sexually explicit conduct involving a minor, including drawn, AI-generated or edited imagery.
  • Sexualised depictions of minors of any kind, including text, audio, "doodles" and captions.
  • Grooming: building a relationship with a minor for the purpose of sexual abuse or exploitation, including attempts to pair with a minor.
  • Sextortion: threatening to share sexual imagery to coerce anyone, including a minor.
  • Minor sex trafficking or the advertising, soliciting or facilitation of sexual abuse of a child.
  • Sharing, requesting, linking to or describing where to find any of the above.

3. DuosApp is for adults

  • DuosApp is designed for adult couples and is rated for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to create an account.
  • There is no discovery, no public profile and no way to find or be found: the only way to connect is a one-time code exchanged between two people who already know each other. This design removes the stranger-contact pathway that most grooming relies on.
  • If we learn that an account belongs to a minor, we close it and delete its data.

4. How to report a concern

Anyone — whether or not they use DuosApp — can report a child safety concern:

  • In the app: Settings → Help & Feedback. You can reach it without leaving the app.
  • By email: duosapp.support@gmail.com with the subject "Child safety report".
  • Directly to authorities: the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) in India, the NCMEC CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org) in the United States, or your local police.

Please do not forward or re-share suspected CSAM when reporting; describe it and tell us where it is.

5. How we respond

  1. Review: child safety reports are read by a person and treated as the highest priority.
  2. Remove: where we have actual knowledge of CSAM or CSAE content, we remove it from our systems.
  3. Terminate: we permanently terminate the accounts involved and may unpair the partner account.
  4. Preserve and report: we preserve the relevant evidence and report CSAM to the appropriate authorities — in India to the national cyber crime authorities under the Information Technology Act and the POCSO Act, and, where applicable, to NCMEC or the relevant national body.
  5. Cooperate: we respond to lawful requests from law enforcement.

6. Compliance with child safety laws

We comply with applicable child safety laws, including the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules in India, and with Google Play's Developer Program Policies, including the Child Safety Standards policy and the Child Endangerment policy. We review these standards at least annually and whenever the law or the app changes materially.

7. Designated child safety contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety matters, able to speak to our CSAE prevention practices and compliance, can be reached at duosapp.support@gmail.com. Law enforcement and child protection agencies should use the same address and mark messages "Child safety — law enforcement".
