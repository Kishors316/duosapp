Community Guidelines

DuosApp is a space for two people. There is no public feed, no audience and no strangers — which means these guidelines are mostly about how you treat the one person on the other side. They apply to everything you do in the app, from chat and doodles to games, the diary and location sharing.

1. The spirit of these guidelines

Everything in DuosApp is shared with someone who trusts you. Add things your partner would be glad to find. Respect their boundaries, their privacy and their right to walk away.

2. Respect and consent

  • Pair only with someone who wants to pair with you. Pairing requires both people to act. Never pressure someone into accepting a code.
  • Location is a gift, not an obligation. Never demand that your partner share their location, and never use location sharing to control, follow or monitor them. Either partner may stop sharing at any time, for any reason, without explanation.
  • Intimate content needs ongoing consent. Only share intimate photos, videos or messages of your partner with their clear agreement, and never share them outside DuosApp or keep them after they ask you to delete them.
  • The shared space is shared. Diary entries, notes and dates can be edited or removed by either partner. Don't use that to erase or rewrite what your partner has added.
  • Games are for fun. Truth or Dare, This or That and the quiz are prompts, not weapons. Don't use answers to humiliate or coerce.

3. What is never allowed

We will remove content and may terminate accounts involved in any of the following:

  • Any sexual content involving a minor, or any attempt to use DuosApp to contact, groom or exploit a child. We report this to the appropriate authorities. See our Child Safety Standards.
  • Users under 18. DuosApp is for adults only.
  • Abuse, threats, harassment or intimidation of your partner, including threats to share private content ("revenge" imagery) or using the app to stalk them.
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery of anyone.
  • Impersonation — creating an account as someone you are not, or pairing with someone by deceiving them about who you are.
  • Illegal content, including material that incites violence or promotes terrorism, and content that infringes someone else's copyright.
  • Attacks on the service — attempting to bypass security rules, access another pairing's data, tamper with app-integrity checks, or obtain Premium without paying.

4. Shared content

  • Only upload content you have the right to share.
  • Keep file sizes and storage reasonable. Free accounts share 2 GB; Premium shares 50 GB.
  • Content catalogues in the app (daily questions, date ideas, game prompts) are ours; don't scrape or republish them.

5. Your safety

  • You can unpair at any time from Settings. Your partner immediately loses access to the shared space going forward.
  • You can stop sharing your location instantly from the Map tab.
  • You can lock the app with a PIN so no one with your phone can open it.
  • You can delete your account and all shared content from Settings → Delete Account. See the Data Deletion Policy.
  • If you are in danger, contact local emergency services. DuosApp is not an emergency service.

6. Reporting a concern

  • In the app: Settings → Help & Feedback. Choose the category that fits and describe what happened.
  • By email: duosapp.support@gmail.com. For child safety concerns, please put "Child safety" in the subject line.

Reports are read by a person. We may ask for more detail. We will not tell your partner that you reported them unless we are legally required to.

7. How we enforce these guidelines

Depending on severity we may remove content, warn you, restrict features, unpair accounts, or terminate an account permanently. Where content is illegal, we preserve evidence and report to the relevant authorities. We don't read private chats to look for violations — we act on reports, on content that is surfaced to us, and on signals from our abuse-prevention systems.
