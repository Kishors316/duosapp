Private by design

Your relationship isn't content

DuosApp was built around one idea: what two people share with each other belongs to them.

  • One partner, one code, one pairing — there is no way to find or be found
  • Chats, photos and dates are readable only by your two accounts, enforced by server-side rules
  • Your chats, photos and dates are never sold and never used for advertising
  • Location sharing is off until you turn it on, and either of you can stop it instantly
  • Delete your account from inside the app and your data goes with it
  • Every request is attested with Play Integrity, so our servers can tell the real app from anything else

Questions

Frequently asked

Is DuosApp free?

Yes. Chat, doodles, games, the daily question, date ideas, the gallery, special dates, location sharing and mood check-ins are free, with 2 GB of shared storage. Premium lifts the diary, notes, to-do and timeline limits, adds 50 GB of storage and unlocks the Secret Vault — one subscription covers both partners. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Can anyone else see our chats?

No. DuosApp has no feed, no followers and no discovery. Your data is readable only by the two paired accounts, enforced by server-side security rules. We don't sell data and we don't show ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. If you can't sign in, email us — the steps are on the Data Deletion Policy page.

Who can use DuosApp?

DuosApp is for adults aged 18 and over. See our Child Safety Standards.
