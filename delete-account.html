Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: September 4, 2026

DuosApp users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting support.

How to request deletion

  1. Email duosapp.support@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line DuosApp Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or other account identifier associated with your DuosApp account so the request can be located.
  4. For security, we may need to verify that you control the account.

What happens next

After verification, the deletion request will be processed and eligible account information and associated user data will be deleted or de-identified, subject to legitimate legal, security, billing, or fraud-prevention retention requirements.

Need help?

Contact duosapp.support@gmail.com.
