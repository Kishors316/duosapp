Data Deletion Policy

You can delete your DuosApp account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what is removed, and how long it takes. It is the account-deletion resource referenced from our Google Play listing.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open DuosApp and go to Profile (bottom tab).
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion runs on our servers immediately and you are signed out. If your partner still has their account, their app keeps working and shows you as "Deleted Account" — see what your partner keeps before you confirm, because it is different from what you might expect.

2. Delete by email if you can't sign in

If you no longer have access to the Google account you signed in with, or the app isn't working for you, email duosapp.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my DuosApp account". To protect your data we will ask you to confirm the request from the email address linked to the account (or otherwise verify that it is yours). We complete verified requests within 7 days and confirm by email.

3. What gets deleted

When your account is deleted we remove, straight away and for good:

  • your account record and sign-in link, and your profile — name, gender, photo, birthday, birth year;
  • your pairing code and any pending pairing requests;
  • your location record and your mood;
  • your push-notification token and presence (online/offline) data;
  • your Premium record on our side (Google keeps its own purchase records — see section 6).

What happens to the shared content — the chat, doodles, moments, diary, notes, to-dos, timeline, special dates, game rounds and the Secret Vault — depends on whether your partner still has an account:

  • Your partner has already deleted their account too: the entire shared space, including every message, photo, video, voice note and vault file, is deleted from our servers at the same time as your account.
  • Your partner still has their account: the shared space stays available to them, with your name replaced by "Deleted Account". It is deleted from our servers when they delete their account. See section 4.

Copies in backups are removed within 30 days. We may retain minimal purchase records (Google Play order identifiers and subscription state) for up to 7 years where required for tax, accounting and fraud prevention; these never include your messages or content.

4. What your partner keeps

A shared space belongs to the pairing, not to one person. Everything in it was sent to, or made with, your partner — so deleting your account does not take it away from them, in the same way that deleting a messaging account does not erase the messages you already sent to someone. While your partner keeps their account, they can still see the chat and the shared space, with you shown as "Deleted Account". Your profile, photo, birthday, location and mood are gone; the things you shared are not.

If there are specific messages, photos or files you want gone before you leave, delete them first — section 5 explains how to delete any item for both of you. Once your account is deleted you can no longer do this, and we cannot do it for you, because the content now belongs to a space you are no longer part of.

Content your partner has already downloaded — photos saved to their gallery, or items cached for offline viewing — is on their device and outside our control, whatever happens to your account. If you need something removed from their device, you will need to ask them.

5. Deleting some data without closing your account

  • Messages: long-press a message to delete it for yourself or for everyone.
  • Doodles, moments, diary entries, notes, to-dos, milestones and special dates: open the item and delete it.
  • Vault files: delete them from the Vault.
  • Location: switch sharing off; the record is deleted at once.
  • Downloaded copies on this phone: Settings → Storage → Clear downloaded copies.
  • Birth year: Settings → Special Dates → turn off "Include the year".
  • Unpair without deleting: Settings → Unpair. Your account stays; the shared space ends.

6. Premium and deletion

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — subscriptions are managed by Google Play, not by us. Cancel it first in Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, otherwise it will keep renewing. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Timing
