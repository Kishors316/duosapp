Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

DuosApp is free to use. Premium is an optional upgrade sold through Google Play. This policy explains how to cancel, when refunds apply, and what happens to Premium if you unpair or delete your account.

1. What Premium is and how it's sold

  • Premium unlocks: 50 GB of shared storage (up from 2 GB), unlimited diary entries, notes, to-dos and timeline milestones, and the Secret Vault.
  • Plans: weekly, monthly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, and a one-time Lifetime purchase.
  • One purchase covers both partners. Premium is applied to the pairing, so only one of you needs to buy it.
  • All purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card details. The price is shown in Google Play before you confirm, in your local currency, and may include applicable taxes.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel. Google Play notifies you before renewal.

2. The free tier

Nothing you created is ever deleted because a subscription ends. If Premium lapses, you keep every diary entry, note, to-do, milestone and file — you simply can't add new ones beyond the free limits until you upgrade again, and the Vault is locked until you upgrade again — the files in it are kept, not deleted. Editing existing items is always free.

3. Cancelling a subscription

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select DuosApp Premium and tap Cancel subscription.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've already paid for.
  • Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Please cancel in Google Play first.
  • Lifetime purchases don't renew and don't need cancelling.

4. Refunds

  • Because purchases are made through Google Play, refunds are issued by Google under the Google Play refund policy. In most cases you can request a refund within 48 hours of a purchase directly from Google Play; after that, Google forwards requests to the developer.
  • We will refund a purchase if Premium did not activate for you and your partner within 24 hours and we can't fix it, if you were charged twice for the same period, or if a renewal was charged after you cancelled in Google Play. Email us with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.).
  • We generally don't refund partial periods of a subscription you simply stopped using, or purchases where the features worked as described. For a Lifetime purchase, the 48-hour Google Play window applies.
  • Refund requests are handled by email within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method by Google and may take several days to appear.

5. Unpairing, deletion and Premium

  • If you unpair, Premium stays with the account that purchased it. If your partner bought it, you return to the free tier when you unpair.
  • If you re-pair with someone new, your Premium covers the new pairing.
  • If you delete your account, any remaining subscription time is forfeited unless you cancel in Google Play first and request a refund under section 4. We can't restore Premium to a deleted account.
  • New phone? Sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen.

6. Price and plan changes

If we change the price of a subscription, Google Play will notify you in advance and the new price only applies after you accept it or at your next renewal, as required by Google Play's rules. If we discontinue a plan, your current period will be honoured.

7. Getting help

Billing questions and refund requests: duosapp.support@gmail.com. Include the Google Play order number and the email address of the account that made the purchase. Nothing in this policy limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (India) or other applicable consumer law.
